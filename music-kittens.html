Small group music classes for little ones.

Sing, move and explore music with Cat. Two classes for different stages of early childhood, from babies to preschoolers.

Find your class

Ages 0–3 · With your caregiver

Music Kittens

Tuesdays, 9:30–10:10am

A gentle introduction to music through singing, rhythm play, movement and time together. A familiar 40-minute routine helps little ones feel confident and ready to join in.

What we’ll do

  • Start with a welcome song, and finish with a calm closing ritual.
  • Move, play and explore scarves, shakers and simple instruments.
  • Practice listening and taking turns through familiar songs and routines.

Caregivers take part in the class with their little ones.

Ages 3–5 · For preschoolers

Mini Musicians

Tuesdays, 2:00–2:40pm

Preschoolers explore music through singing, movement and hands-on instrument play. Each 40-minute class introduces musical ideas in a structured, engaging routine made for young children.

What we’ll do

  • Sing welcome songs and express music through movement.
  • Try instruments and explore rhythm, pitch and listening.
  • Discover musical words, symbols and ideas through familiar routines.

Caregivers are required to stay on site during class.

Join us this fall

Each class is limited to six families. Enrollment is first come, first served.
